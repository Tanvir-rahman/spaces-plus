Spaces+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Spaces+ is a Chrome extension that shows your tabs in a sidebar organized into spaces.

What Spaces+ uses

To work, Spaces+ reads the titles, addresses and icons of your open tabs, your tab groups, the bookmarks it creates in its own "Spaces+" folder, your recently closed tabs and your downloads list. It uses these only to show them in the sidebar and to act when you click.

What is stored

Your spaces, settings and a backup list of each space's open tabs are saved on your own computer using Chrome's extension storage. Favorites and pinned tabs are saved as Chrome bookmarks, which Chrome may sync to your Google account if you have bookmark sync turned on.

What is shared

Nothing. Spaces+ has no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It never sends your data anywhere and never sells or transfers it to anyone.

Deleting your data

Use Customize → Start over to delete all spaces, pinned tabs, favorites and the tab backup, or remove the extension to delete everything it stored.
