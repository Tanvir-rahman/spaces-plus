**Title:** Spaces+ — Vertical tabs and spaces for Chrome

**Description:**
Too many tabs? Spaces+ puts them in a clean vertical sidebar, sorted into spaces for work, study and life.

• Spaces with their own colour, icon, pinned tabs and open tabs
• Split view: drop one tab onto another
• Any width, from a 48 px icon strip to a roomy sidebar
• Your look: colours, sizes, dark mode
• ⌘K / Ctrl+K finds any tab, pin or bookmark
• Library with downloads and closed tabs, and your tabs come back after a restart

Free. No account. No tracking.
